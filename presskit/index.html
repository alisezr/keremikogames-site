About the game

Kai: Medallion Chase is a hard but fair 2D ninja platformer. One morning Kai's medallion is gone, and the trail leads deep into the canyon. From there the journey crosses tropical islands and a desert full of pyramids, with traps, enemies and boss fights at every turn.

All 90 levels across three worlds, the Canyon, the Tropical islands and the Desert, were designed by hand, with one goal: when you die, you should know exactly what went wrong. There are three playable heroes: Kai fights with a katana that can parry and cut arrows out of the air, Rohin brings a sword, a shield and a ground-shaking strike, and Velmor fights with lightning, spells and portals. The story is told through fully voiced cinematics. Most levels hide secret rooms, every level has its own world record leaderboard, and a separate survival mode, Arrow Storm, asks a simple question: how long can you last?

Features

  • 90 hand-crafted levels in three worlds: Canyon, Tropical and Desert
  • Three playable heroes: Kai, Rohin and Velmor, each with their own skills
  • Fully voiced cinematics in five languages
  • Katana combat with combos and parries, including cutting arrows mid-air
  • Unlockable skills and a training ground that teaches each one
  • Hidden rooms and secret gold in most levels
  • A world record leaderboard for every level, timed to the hundredth of a second
  • Arrow Storm survival mode

About the developer

KeremikoGames is Ali Sezer, a solo developer from Turkey. Kai is the studio's first game, and Ali learned Unity while making it. The 1.1.0 update added two more worlds, Tropical and Desert, bringing the game to 90 levels.
