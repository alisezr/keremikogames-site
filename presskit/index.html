About the game

Kai: Medallion Chase is a hard but fair 2D ninja platformer. One morning Kai's medallion is gone, and the trail leads deep into the canyon. The game gives you full control the whole time: it isn't an auto-runner, so you can stop, line up a jump and take it when you're ready.

All 30 levels of the first world were designed by hand, with one goal: when you die, you should know exactly what went wrong. Kai fights with a katana that can parry and cut arrows out of the air, and gold collected along the way unlocks four skills. Most levels hide secret rooms, every level has its own world record leaderboard, and a separate survival mode, Arrow Storm, asks a simple question: how long can you last?

Features

  • 30 hand-crafted levels in the Canyon world
  • Katana combat with combos and parries, including cutting arrows mid-air
  • Four unlockable skills (Kunai, Tobi, Kage, Homura) and a training ground that teaches each one
  • Hidden rooms and secret gold in most levels
  • A world record leaderboard for every level, timed to the hundredth of a second
  • Arrow Storm survival mode
  • Fully voiced in five languages

About the developer

KeremikoGames is Ali Sezer, a solo developer from Turkey. Kai is the studio's first game, and Ali learned Unity while making it. Two more worlds, Tropical and Desert, are planned, which would bring the game to 90 levels.
